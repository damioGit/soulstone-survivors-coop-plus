Shared levels, tougher enemies and connection fixes. Game version 1.5.3.2.

**Everyone needs 0.11.0**: it does not connect to 0.10.0 or older.

## New

- **One shared level for the whole group**: all XP goes into one pool, so everyone levels up at the same moment. XP bonuses (runes, items, upgrades) count for the team at 1/N each: with 2 players a bonus counts half, with 4 a quarter (the team's bonus is the average of everyone's).
- **Tougher enemies**: `[Coop] EnemyHealthBonus` now defaults to +100 % enemy health per extra player (+200 % with 3); a config below 100 is raised once.
- **Camera zoom-out in co-op**: the camera sits 25 % farther in co-op runs, so you see more around you and your partners. **Co-op options → Camera zoom out** (off / 25 % / 50 %, `[Coop] CameraZoomOut`). Solo play keeps the game's camera.

## Fixed

- **Joining over Steam sometimes asked for a lobby code**: the host refused a joiner whose first message arrived a moment before Steam listed it in the lobby. The host now waits up to 10 s for the lobby to catch up.
- **A guest's run ended when the host's game hung for a few seconds**: Steam dropped the session after ~10 s of silence and the guest gave up at once. The session now reopens by itself, and a player is only dropped after 30 s without a word.
- **Ores and healing crystals for guests**: the copper/iron ores and the healing crystals never reached the guests (they have no team, and the host skipped them). Guests now have them in the world and on the minimap, and can break them.
- **Continuous boss attacks on guests** (Slayer's Bladestorm, Avatar of Fire's flamethrower...): the guest saw only the first 1.5 s while the attack kept hitting. A channeled attack now stays visible until it ends on the host.

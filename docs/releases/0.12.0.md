Guests can fight in Mastery, boss beams and cleaves for guests, fog that follows the zoom, partners in the menu. Game version 1.5.3.2.

**Everyone needs 0.12.0**: it does not connect to 0.11.0 or older.

## New

- **Partners in the menu** (experimental): the connected players' chosen characters stand next to yours in the main menu, with their names over them, and change as soon as they pick another character, skin or weapon. **Co-op options → Partners in the menu** (`[Coop] LobbyPartners`).

## Fixed

- **Guests couldn't attack in Mastery** (the Cathedral): their character aimed at enemies but never cast. The game only lets players auto-cast while combat is on, and it turns combat on in the boss spawn that only the host runs. Guests now follow the host's combat state.
- **Invisible boss attacks for guests**: charged line attacks were never shown to guests, while their hits still landed. That covered 21 attacks, including the flamethrower of the Avatar of Fire, the rays of Medusa and the Cultist Sorcerer, the Mechanical Golem's energy beam and the bosses' cleaves. They now show with their warning area. If one ever fails on a guest, only that attack is skipped from then on.
- **Ordinary enemies' attacks farther away** (tornadoes, pools and so on) now reach guests from 45 m instead of 30 m, so the zoomed-out camera doesn't show them popping in.
- **Fog with the zoomed-out camera**: at 50 % the map drowned in fog. The fog distances now move back with the camera.
- **Log spam in the menus**: the camera zoom wrote a warning every frame outside a run (hundreds of MB of log).

The Cathedral (Mastery) works for guests: no more frame drops, the boss arena loads for everyone, guests see Mh'thaeus, his attacks and the lords' statues, and their summons fight. The party panel shows each partner's prestige. Game version 1.5.3.2.

**Everyone needs 0.14.0**: it does not connect to 0.13.0 or older.

## New

- **Prestige stars**: the party panel shows each partner's prestige for the character they play, as the game's own stars under their portrait (the game gives the first star at prestige 10).

## Fixed

- **Low frame rate in the Cathedral and in the menu**:
  - In Mastery, the host's game spent more than half of every frame looking for the Mastery portal and manager.
  - In the main menu, both players' games spent more than half of every frame looking for the character stand.
  - The mod now takes about 5% of a frame in a run and 1% in the menu. The short stutters every half second (chests, quests, boss statues) are also gone.
  - During the finale, guests no longer rescan the whole map for each change the host sends, and the host no longer mirrors its own interface (damage flash, upgrade highlights) to the guests.
- **Black arena for guests**: when Mh'thaeus pulls the players into his arena, guests now load the arena too and are moved there once it has loaded. Before, they landed in an empty black space with an invisible floor and no boss.
- **Mh'thaeus for guests**:
  - Guests now see him: his body, tentacles and void effects, which he turns on during the fight.
  - Guests see the attacks he gains during the fight, including the rotating rays.
  - His health bar follows the host's through every phase. It used to stop at 60%.
- **Lords' statues in Mastery**: guests saw the purple statues for 12 seconds only; they now stand as long as the host's.
- **Guests' summons stuck on the sleeping boss**: in Mastery, the main boss waits untargetable until the finale. For guests it was a normal target, so their summons and auto-aim went for it the whole run.

## Checked

- **Guests' damage**: everything a guest hits reaches the host. In a test with the same character and build, the guest's damage was at least the host's, also with 120 ms of ping.
- **The purple pentagram (cycle modifiers)**: the same for host and guest in Mastery and after Endless portals.

## Known limits

- **The Cathedral is still a work in progress**: the finale was tested in a scripted run, not yet in a full real one ([#1](https://github.com/damioGit/soulstone-survivors-coop-plus/issues/1)).
- **Titan Hunt (Crusade) is a work in progress**: not tested in this release ([#2](https://github.com/damioGit/soulstone-survivors-coop-plus/issues/2)).

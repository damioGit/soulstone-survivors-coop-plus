First public build of **Soulstone Coop**, an online co-op mod for Soulstone Survivors (game version 1.5.3.2).

> **Pre-release.** Everything up to 0.7.0 was played by two people over Steam; the 0.8.0 changes below compile but have not been played yet.

## Install

Download `SoulstoneCoop-0.8.0.zip`, extract it into the game folder (Steam → right-click the game → Manage → Browse local files) so that `winhttp.dll` and `BepInEx/` sit next to `Soulstone Survivors.exe`, and launch from Steam. The first launch takes a few minutes. Full steps are in `README.txt` (English) and `LEIA-ME.txt` (Portuguese) inside the zip.

Both players need the same mod version: 0.8.0 does not connect to 0.7.0.

## New since 0.7.0

- **Your partner looks like your partner:** their own character, skin and weapon, with their Steam name, a health bar, an ally ring and a minimap marker.
- **Boss counter on the guest** follows the host (it used to stay at 0/N).
- **Level-up without pausing the session** once your build is complete (`[Coop] LevelUpPause`).
- **Endless and challenge portals, and Restart,** take both players to the same next stage.
- **Disconnects:** if the host leaves, the guest gets a notice and returns to the menu; if the guest leaves, the host keeps playing. A "connection unstable" warning shows before a drop.
- God Meteors show on the guest; slows and stuns reach the guest; replayed hits no longer show duplicate damage numbers; fixed a boss skill that could hit the guest twice.
- Fixed large enemy snapshots failing over direct IP with more than ~90 enemies.

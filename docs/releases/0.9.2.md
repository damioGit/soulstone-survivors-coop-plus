Level-ups that never pause, partners' dash trail, and the mod's texts in your own language. Game version 1.5.3.2.

**Everyone needs 0.9.2**: it does not connect to 0.9.1 or older. Install the same way: extract `SoulstoneCoop-0.9.2.zip` into the game folder over the previous version.

## New

- **Every upgrade choice uses the compact panel, with no time pause in co-op** (#1): level-ups, queued level-ups, elite/boss power-ups, chest rewards, rerolls/banish/lock/skip and skill swaps. You keep moving and attacking while you pick (1-4, Tab hides it). With a gamepad, the left stick keeps moving and the D-pad picks. The old `[Coop] LevelUpPause` setting is gone, so a value saved by 0.8.x no longer brings the pause back. Esc still pauses everyone.
- **Partners' dash** now shows the game's dash trail on their character, in their character's color (#2).
- **The mod speaks your language:** every text the mod shows follows your game's language setting, and changes as soon as you switch it in Options. English and Brazilian Portuguese are hand-written; Spanish, French, German, Russian, Chinese and Japanese are machine-translated for now (corrections welcome). `[Coop] Language` forces one.

Fixes and missing co-op pieces, plus a redesigned Co-op panel. Game version 1.5.3.2.

**Everyone needs 0.10.0**: it does not connect to 0.9.2 or older.

## New

- **Co-op panel in the game's own style**: it mirrors the character panel on the left of the hub, with the game's buttons, title frame and fields; it only shows what makes sense in each state. The tab bar is narrowed so the Co-op tab no longer sits under the options gear.
- **Upgrade choices pause the whole group again**: with team XP everyone levels up together, so the game waits until all have picked (each on the game's own panel); the others see who they're waiting for. A pick that takes over 30 s turns into the small panel and the rest play on. `[Coop] PauseOnLevelUp = false` keeps 0.9.2's no-pause overlay.
- **Guests' crowd control works**: their slows, weakness/fragility and other statuses, stuns and knockback reach the real enemies (only their damage did before).
- **Chests and map tasks for everyone**: guests see the reward chests and the map tasks' triggers (with minimap icons), open and start them by standing in their circle, and get the task's start/finish notices; the loot is shared as usual.
- **Boss statues** (the purple figures bosses rise from) show on guests.
- **Enemies move smoothly on guests**: they keep up with the host's (faster, buffed) enemies instead of lagging and snapping back.
- **Regular enemies attack visibly on guests** (bites, shots), not only bosses, elites and specials. Throttled: nearby enemies only, a few KB/s at most.
- **Heal orbs reach every player**, and **guests get their materials** (copper, iron...): every player gets its own roll, as the game does for several players.
- **Tougher enemies in co-op**: `[Coop] EnemyHealthBonus` (default 50) gives every enemy +50 % health per extra player (+100 % with 3). Set 100 for harder runs, 0 for the game's own numbers.
- **Guests dodge by dashing** like when playing alone (`[Coop] DashDodge`, on by default).
- **Partners' rotating shields** (always-on skills) and the live aim of channeled skills are shown.
- **God skills bosses make the God-Entity cast** show on guests.
- **A downed partner lies down** on your screen; partners' minimap icons have their own color; their walk animation follows their own speed.
- **Mastery mode in co-op**: the portal, cycles, Lords, objectives, pillars, timer and boss health bars stay in step, every player picks their own powers each cycle, and the final boss works for everyone. When it pulls the host into its arena, guests are brought there too (they also see the arena), and the win counts for all.
- **Titan Hunt in co-op**: the whole chain works for the group. The host picks the titan and everyone goes to that map with their level, items and the vial; the guest sees the three titans on offer on its own panel and its press is a proposal the host sees ("B proposes: ..."). Key fragments, the door, the boss room (whoever is outside is brought in), the titan blood (whoever takes it takes it for all), the sigils, the Primordial Portal and the fortress runes follow the host, and the guest can work all of them; walking into a portal asks the host to take it for everyone. The final map's titan and the Void King (all three stages, the crown) end in a victory for everyone; their cutscenes play on the host and the others wait with a banner.
- **Co-op options in the panel**: a **Co-op options** button folds out the host's settings (enemy health and numbers per player, joining mid-run, lobby type and size) and your own (pause on level-up, dash dodge, ending the run when the host leaves), saved as you click.
- **From the author's 1.1.0**: the party panel at the top left of a run (each partner's health, level, XP, revives left and state; `[Coop] PartyHud`), the compact upgrade panel above the HUD with gamepad support (`[Coop] LevelUpPanelPosition`), and guests seeing every enemy type the host spawns (#6).
- **Polish** texts.

## Fixed

- Guests built the stage with their own curses, so boss counters and modifiers could differ from the host's (#3).
- Tutorial hints and the visibility notice froze every player; in co-op they no longer pause anyone (Esc still does).
- Over Steam, a guest who quit and rejoined within ~15 seconds got no player slot.
